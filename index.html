ARTIST

ELASRI THRone

ELASRI THRone's paintings are an ongoing dialogue with the light and topography of the American Southwest. Based in the high deserts of New Mexico, she translates the vast, silent landscapes into textured, minimalist compositions.

Her work explores the intersection of static memory and fluid movement, balancing architectural structural integrity with a deliberate, geometric calm.

Thorne's work is held in prestigious private and museum collections across four continents, continuing a legacy of contemporary Southwestern abstraction.
